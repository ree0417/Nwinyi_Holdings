About Nwinyi Holdings

Delivering Quality Uniforms Across South Africa

Who We Are

Nwinyi Holdings Pty Ltd is a proudly South African manufacturer and supplier of school uniforms, corporate wear, workwear and safety clothing. Established in 2017, we have built a reputation for producing durable, comfortable and professionally designed uniforms for schools, businesses, government institutions and private clients.

Our goal is to provide affordable, high-quality uniforms while contributing to local job creation and the growth of South Africa's textile industry.

Our Vision

To be a leading national supplier of high-quality school and corporate uniforms proudly made in South Africa.

Our Mission

To manufacture and deliver uniforms that reflect professionalism, comfort and durability while supporting local employment and skills development.
